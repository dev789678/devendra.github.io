THREAT AWARENESS

Common Cyber Threats

Recognize warning signs and practice simple prevention habits.

🎣

Phishing

What it is: Fake emails, messages or websites designed to trick people into giving information.

Safety: Check the sender, URL and request independently.

🦠

Malware

What it is: Malicious software that can damage systems or steal information.

Safety: Keep software updated and download only from trusted sources.

🔒

Ransomware

What it is: Malware that can block access to data and demand payment.

Safety: Maintain backups and keep devices and security software updated.

🪪

Identity Theft

What it is: Misuse of personal information to impersonate or defraud someone.

Safety: Limit personal data sharing and protect account credentials.

😡

Cyberbullying

What it is: Repeated online behavior intended to threaten, humiliate or harm others.

Safety: Block/report harmful behavior and keep evidence when appropriate.

💳

Online Fraud

What it is: Deceptive schemes involving money, shopping, jobs or investments.

Safety: Verify offers and never share OTPs, PINs or passwords.

🔑

Password Attacks

What it is: Attempts to guess, steal or reuse credentials.

Safety: Use unique passwords and multi-factor authentication.

Golden rule: If a message creates panic, urgency or pressure to act immediately, slow down and verify it.
