20 PRINCIPLES

20 Cyber Ethics Rules

Core principles for safe, respectful and responsible internet use.

01

Respect others

Use polite language and respect different views and cultures.

02

Protect personal information

Do not publicly share passwords, OTPs, addresses or sensitive data.

03

Use strong passwords

Create long, unique passwords for important accounts.

04

Use MFA

Enable multi-factor authentication wherever available.

05

Think before posting

Consider the future impact of photos, comments and posts.

06

Do not cyberbully

Never threaten, harass or repeatedly target another person online.

07

Verify information

Check reliable sources before sharing important claims.

08

Respect copyright

Use content legally and credit creators when required.

09

Avoid plagiarism

Acknowledge sources and submit original work.

10

Beware of phishing

Check senders, links and requests before responding.

11

Update devices

Install security updates and patches from trusted sources.

12

Use secure networks

Be careful with sensitive activity on public or unknown Wi-Fi.

13

Download responsibly

Use trusted stores and sources for files and apps.

14

Respect digital property

Never access or modify another person's data without permission.

15

Report harmful behavior

Use reporting tools for scams, abuse and serious violations.

16

Protect your digital footprint

Review public posts and privacy settings regularly.

17

Do not impersonate

Do not create deceptive accounts to mislead people.

18

Use technology lawfully

Follow laws, school rules and platform policies.

19

Maintain healthy screen habits

Take breaks and balance online and offline activities.

20

Be a responsible digital citizen

Use technology to learn, create, collaborate and help others.
